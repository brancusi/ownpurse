## What it is

FloQast is close management and compliance software for accounting teams, founded in Los Angeles in 2013 by Mike Whitmire, a CPA who had run month-end closes himself. It sits beside the ERP and organises the close the way a controller already does: a checklist, reconciliations that a preparer prepares and a reviewer signs off, journal entries that are reviewed before they post, and audit work. It passed [$200M in annual recurring revenue](https://www.floqast.com/press-releases/floqast-hits-200-million-arr) on 20 January 2026 and says more than 3,500 accounting teams use it.

## How it works

FloQast connects to [NetSuite, SAP, Workday, Microsoft Dynamics, Sage Intacct and Xero](https://floqast.com/integrations/) and pulls each account's balance. On the [reconciliations page](https://www.floqast.com/close-management/products/account-reconciliations), each account gets a preparer, a reviewer and a due date; support (bank statements, subledger detail, schedules) is stored with it; reviewers raise notes and preparers clear them, and "strict sign-off keeps the two roles separate." If the GL shifts after sign-off, owners are alerted so sign-offs do not go stale, and a completeness check confirms every account was reconciled. Auto sign-off is limited to unchanged and zero-balance accounts. [Journal Entry Management](https://www.floqast.com/close-ai/products/journal-entry-management) creates, validates and posts journals to the ERP, requires documentation, and locks approved entries.

## Data and access

FloQast has an API, but a narrow one. Its help centre has a section on building to the FloQast API covering API keys tied to a user account, an API audit log, and subledger tie-out and matching endpoints; the pages are not public. Third parties sell unified or unofficial access, which says the official surface does not cover sign-offs and attachments. Pricing is by quote, in three packages (Close Management, Close AI, Risk and Compliance), with [no per-user fees](https://floqast.com/pricing/). Close data can be exported in a pivot-friendly format.

## Agents

FloQast launched [auditable AI agents](https://www.floqast.com/press-releases/floqast-launches-auditable-ai-agents-to-bridge-the-talent-gap-and-elevate-accountants-from-preparers-to-strategic-reviewers) on 25 March 2025: a journal entry agent, a data transformation agent and custom agents, built in FloQast Transform. In [September 2026](https://www.floqast.com/press-releases/floqast-takecontrol-2026-ai-accounting-innovations) Transform learned to build an agent from a completed close, test it against numbers the team already approved, and document it for the auditor. Teams sign off on every step before an agent goes live, and an AI review of a journal leaves "the human" to make every call. FloQast is ISO 42001 certified and is building a COSO generative-AI governance module. There is no MCP server or outside agent interface in its press releases through 16 September 2026; the agents live inside FloQast.

## Customers

Customers include Lululemon, Chipotle, Shopify, Twilio, Zoom and the Los Angeles Lakers. Growth runs through partners: EY, Deloitte Australia, PwC Belgium, RSM and Forvis Mazars. Review-site ratings could not be read in this pass.

## History

FloQast raised a [$110M Series D at a $1.2B valuation](https://builtin.com/articles/floqast-unicorn-raise-110m-1b-valuation-hiring) in July 2021, with $203M raised by then and 1,400 customers, and a [$100M Series E at $1.6B](https://floqast.com/press/floqast-secures-100-million-in-series-e-funding-achieving-1-6-billion-valuation/) led by ICONIQ Growth on 10 April 2024. Customer counts went from 1,400 (2021) to 2,600 (April 2024) to 3,500 (September 2025).

## For ownpurse

FloQast is the reference for what sign-off means to a controller, and ownpurse should match it: two roles that cannot be the same person, notes that are raised and cleared, a sign-off that goes stale when the balance under it moves, and a completeness check before the period closes. FloQast's agent story (agents propose, people approve, every step logged) is ownpurse's principle, applied inside one vendor's cloud. Its weak side is access: a gated, partial API and no MCP. ownpurse can bring the same review discipline to a small business or an accountant's clients, with the sign-offs and evidence in a record the owner holds and any agent can read.
