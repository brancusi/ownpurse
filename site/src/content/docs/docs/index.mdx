import { CardGrid, LinkCard } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

ownpurse keeps a local record of the facts from your financial sources, in a file you hold. It rebuilds your ledger
from that record, checks it against the source to the cent, and lets you and your agents plan changes that reach your
books only after you approve them. Xero is the first source; others are planned.

ownpurse is in early development. macOS is the tested platform.
